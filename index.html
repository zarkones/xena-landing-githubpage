Why XENA?

XENA C2 empowers you with a versatile post-exploitation agent and a customizable command and control server to simulate a stealthy, persistent adversary within your target network. Its flexible C2 infrastructure allows you to adapt network communications, blending seamlessly with legitimate traffic to evade detection. These capabilities enhance XENA’s streamlined red team operations, cross-platform accessibility across desktop, web, and mobile interfaces, and its open-source framework, fostering community-driven innovation for advanced penetration testing and adversary simulation.
